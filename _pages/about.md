Hello! I am **Jialiang Jin**, a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Structural Engineering, College of Civil Engineering at **Tongji University**. I received my Ph.D. in Structural Engineering from the Graduate School of Environmental Studies at **Nagoya University**, Japan.

My research focuses on improving the seismic resilience of **steel structures**, **steel-concrete composite structures**, **Glass curtain wall systems**, and **metal additive manufacturing (3D printing) structures**. Current topics include innovative irregular steel beam-to-CFST column connections, seismic retrofit and post-earthquake repair, collapse assessment of multi-story steel buildings, and the deformation compatibility of glass curtain walls. I am also collaborating with colleagues to explore experimental research on metal additively manufactured structural components.

I have experience in multi-scale experimental and numerical research, ranging from materials and beam-column subassemblages to complete structural systems. During my doctoral studies, I served as one of the lead student researchers in two large-scale shake-table testing programs, including the **full-scale 10-story steel building test at E-Defense, NIED**, the world's largest three-dimensional structural testing facilities.

Having studied and conducted research in both China and Japan, including six years in Japan, I enjoy working in international and multidisciplinary environments. I am comfortable communicating in Chinese, Japanese, and English, and I appreciate the diverse perspectives that cross-cultural collaboration brings to research.

Research Interests
======

- Metal additive manufacturing components
- Seismic behavior and resilience of steel structures
- Steel-concrete composite structures and CFST column joints
- Prefabricated beam-to-column connections
- Seismic retrofit and post-earthquake repair
- Collapse assessment of multi-story and high-rise steel buildings
- Seismic performance of glass curtain walls
- Large-scale structural testing and advanced finite element analysis

中文简介

金家良，博士，现为同济大学土木工程学院建筑工程系博士后，博士毕业于日本名古屋大学环境学研究科结构工程专业。主要从事钢结构及钢-混凝土组合结构的抗震韧性、加固与震后修复研究，目前正开展3D打印钢结构和钢结构-玻璃幕墙体系抗震性能研究。研究内容涉及新型异型梁柱节点、既有钢结构加固与震后修复、多高层钢结构抗震及抗倒塌性能评估，以及玻璃幕墙的抗震性能与变形机理等，并具有材料、构件和结构体系多尺度试验与数值分析的研究经验。博士期间，作为主要学生代表参与了两项大型振动台试验，包括在日本防灾科学技术研究所E-Defense开展的足尺十层钢结构振动台试验。曾获日本科学技术振兴机构“次世代研究员挑战研究计划”，获评年度优秀研究员，并参与多项日本文部科学省重点科研课题。博士后阶段，主持国家自然科学基金青年科学基金项目（C类）、上海市白玉兰人才计划浦江项目（A类）和国家地震工程科学中心开放课题基金等科研项目，参与国家自然科学基金、国家地震工程科学中心开放课题基金等纵横向科研项目4项。入选教育部博士后海外引才专项、国家资助博士后研究人员计划、上海市博士后海外引进项目、上海市浦江人才及上海市“超级博士后”激励计划等多项人才计划。近五年来，共发表学术论文50余篇，包括SCI JCR一区和二区期刊论文20余篇、EI论文2篇、国际会议论文10篇（多数被EI收录）以及日本建筑学会年会论文22篇。其中，以第一作者或独立通讯作者发表SCI期刊论文14篇，以第二作者身份发表6篇；参编行业规范1部。受邀担任《Engineering Structures》《Journal of Building Engineering》《Journal of Constructional Steel Research》《Scientific Reports》《Buildings》《Applied Sciences》等SCI期刊的审稿人。
